How it works

Three steps. No studio, no separate app for editing sound.

01
Import your clip
Pull in footage straight from your camera roll — however it was shot, wherever it was shot.
02
SmartReel cleans it
Background noise is removed and your voice is leveled automatically, in the time it takes to make coffee.
03
Export and post
Save the cleaned version back to your camera roll, ready for Reels, TikTok, or wherever it's headed next.
What it does

Built around the two things that quietly wreck a good clip.

Background noise removal
Traffic, AC hum, room echo, café chatter — gone, without dulling your actual voice.
Speaker volume isolation
Levels out quiet and loud moments in the same clip, so viewers never have to reach for the volume button.
One-tap processing
No sliders, no presets to learn. Import, wait a moment, export.
Keeps your original
Your raw take is never overwritten — the cleaned version saves alongside it.
Why it matters

People forgive a shaky shot. They rarely forgive bad audio.

Viewers decide whether to keep watching in the first couple of seconds — and muddy, inconsistent sound is one of the fastest ways to lose that decision, even when the visual is great.
— the problem SmartReel is built to solve
